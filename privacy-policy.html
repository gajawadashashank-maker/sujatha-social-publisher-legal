This Privacy Policy explains how Sujatha Social Publisher (“the app”, “we”, “us”) processes information when used by the SRR Bazaar operator to prepare product promotional videos and publish selected content to Instagram. The app is operated for SRR Bazaar.

Important scope note

The app does not connect to or read an existing personal WhatsApp group. The planned WhatsApp integration receives messages and media sent to the WhatsApp Business Platform phone number configured for the app. Forwarding an item to that business number is a separate action by the sender.

1. Information we process

  • WhatsApp message information: when WhatsApp integration is enabled, we may receive message identifiers, timestamps, sender/recipient identifiers, message type, message caption or text, and media identifiers provided in webhook events.
  • Product media: product photos sent to the configured business number, downloaded for processing, and generated poster/video files.
  • Product details: product name, price and description supplied for a promotional Reel.
  • Instagram publishing information: the connected Instagram account identifier, media/container identifiers, captions, publishing status and technical responses needed to publish and troubleshoot a Reel.
  • Technical records: application logs and job identifiers used to operate the service, prevent duplicate processing and diagnose failures.

Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, government identifiers or other sensitive personal information through this app.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to receive product submissions sent to the configured WhatsApp Business number, retrieve submitted media, generate product promotional videos, select one generated video, upload the selected video for Instagram to retrieve, publish it to the connected Instagram account, send operational confirmations where enabled, and maintain security and troubleshooting records.

3. Services and sharing

Information may be processed by the services needed to provide the workflow:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform and Instagram Graph API): receives and delivers messages, media events and publishing requests under Meta's platform terms and privacy policies.
  • Cloudinary: receives the selected generated video so it can be hosted at a URL accessible to Instagram during Reel publishing. The app's current publishing flow uploads the selected video, not all four generated videos.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: when the app is deployed to a cloud server, that provider may store or process application data and logs necessary to operate the app. The provider will be identified in the app's operational configuration.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share submitted product media for unrelated advertising purposes.

4. Storage and retention

Product images, generated videos and job records may be stored on the system running the app. The current workflow keeps generated Reel files in its output folders for operator review. Uploaded source images and webhook-received images may remain in their respective storage folders until the operator deletes them or a retention process is introduced. The selected video is uploaded to Cloudinary; its availability and deletion are also subject to Cloudinary's account settings and terms.

We do not currently promise a fixed automatic deletion period. We retain information only as needed to operate the workflow, maintain records and address failures, and will process reasonable deletion requests as described on the Data Deletion page, subject to legal or platform requirements.

5. Security

Access to app credentials and business accounts should be restricted to authorized operators. The operator should keep access tokens and API secrets private and use HTTPS for public webhooks and hosted services. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may ask us what personal information associated with your interaction is held, request correction, or request deletion. See Data Deletion Instructions or email sujathagajawada1@gmail.com.

7. Children

This app is intended for business/product-content operations and is not designed for children. Do not knowingly submit children's personal information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app's functionality, integrations or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
