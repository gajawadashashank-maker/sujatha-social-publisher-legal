Data Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of personal information processed by Sujatha Social Publisher. These instructions cover information associated with WhatsApp submissions and the app's product-content and Instagram publishing workflow.

How to submit a request

  1. Email sujathagajawada1@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request – Sujatha Social Publisher.
  2. Include the phone number used to message the configured WhatsApp Business number, with country code, and the approximate date/time of the interaction. If relevant, include the WhatsApp message ID or app job ID.
  3. Tell us whether you are requesting deletion of a WhatsApp submission, product image/video, job record or other information.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account or information concerned. Do not email passwords, access tokens or government identity documents.

What happens after a request

We will review the request, locate information reasonably identifiable from the details supplied, and delete or anonymize information we control where appropriate and legally permitted. We will communicate if we need additional information or if some information must be retained for legal, security, dispute-resolution or platform-compliance reasons.

Copies held by service providers

Some information may have been processed by Meta or Cloudinary as part of their services. Deletion from our app does not automatically delete information held independently by those providers. You may also need to use their own privacy or account tools to submit a request to them.

Processing time

We will review and respond to verified requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law. If a request is complex or additional time is needed, we will explain the reason for the delay where appropriate.

Contact

Data requests: sujathagajawada1@gmail.com.

Operator note

This page provides a contact-based deletion process. The app operator must review the request and delete the corresponding local files, job records and any controlled hosted copies. This page is not an automated deletion callback endpoint.
